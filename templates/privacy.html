Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you log: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide and improve Spendly: showing your expenses, building category and monthly summaries, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal and expense data within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for hosting, fonts and basic analytics. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their services and are not permitted to use it for other purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to access, correct or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
